Read the reasoning.
  1. Before: only the actual marks already committed.
  2. Explore: select a pooled candidate combination. Green outlined lamps are hypothetical; red outlines show direct conflicts. Nothing is committed.
  3. Common facts: green outlines show the verified actions to submit. Existing marks remain visible until the actions are committed.
  4. After: the actual deduplicated Lamp/Mark actions and their automatic light.
Generic action replay only enables Before and After. It can display notes, corrections and final-coordinate layouts, but does not invent proofs. Select a configured puzzle/solution from the catalog, or import your own files.
A fast mark contains at least two independent proofs sharing one before-state. Selecting another component always resets its assumptions; it never chains the components.
Click a center to highlight its legal enumeration scope. Click a cell to inspect it; use arrow keys to navigate. Colors have accompanying labels and symbols.
The viewer independently checks legal continuations and strict proof claims. An invalid or unverified suffix is excluded from playback. It does not check minimum brain cost.
